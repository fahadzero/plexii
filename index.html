Uganda's extractive industries sector continues to grow, with significant developments in both mining and oil & gas exploration. The country's mineral wealth includes gold, tin, tungsten, cobalt, and rare earth elements.

Latest Developments

The government has implemented new regulatory frameworks to ensure sustainable and responsible extraction of natural resources. These reforms aim to increase transparency and accountability in the sector.

Recent geological surveys have identified new mineral deposits across multiple regions, opening up opportunities for exploration and development.

Industry Reports

Our comprehensive reports provide detailed analysis of commodity markets, production trends, and policy developments affecting Uganda's extractive industries.
